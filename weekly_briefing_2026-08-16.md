## Electoral Environment

The national picture held steady: Democrats continue to lead the generic ballot by roughly 5–6 points, and this week's forecaster movement was event-driven rather than a shift in mood. Cook's three House downgrades (OH-10, OH-15, CO-03) and Sabato's Wisconsin and Ohio moves all pushed the same direction the environment has run all summer — safe or lean-R seats sliding one notch toward competitiveness — with no forecaster reversing a seat back toward Republicans.

The week's real action was on the primary calendar.
