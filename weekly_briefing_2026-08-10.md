## Electoral Environment

The national picture is unchanged from midsummer: Democrats continue to lead the generic congressional ballot by roughly 5–6 points, and forecaster movement this week was idiosyncratic rather than environmental. Both moves that touched the sheet — Kansas Senate and Texas Governor — were *safe seats sliding one notch toward competitiveness*, not competitive seats changing hands, and both were triggered by concrete events (a Democratic nominee emerging in Kansas; Abbott's polling settling into the mid-single digits in Texas) rather than a shift in the mood.

The week's real action was on the primary calendar, not the ratings board. Michigan (Aug 4) resolved two marquee open contests, Kansas (Aug 4) set its Senate matchup, and Wisconsin, Minnesota, and South Carolina all vote **Aug 11**. Inside Elections used the post-primary clarity to nudge a cluster of Iowa, Kansas, Georgia, North Carolina, and Michigan races into finer "Tilt" gradations — directionally consistent with the D+5–6 environment, but too fine-grained to map onto the dashboard's 7-point scale without a methodology call (logged this week; see Verification).
